I tested three zero-shot prompts for sentiment analysis, product descriptions, and data extraction using 5, 10, and 15 runs. The initial prompts produced different formats, invented product details, and confused item numbers with order numbers. Clear instructions improved sentiment labels and JSON extraction. Few-shot examples combined with stronger factual constraints helped product descriptions avoid unsupported claims. Step-by-step extraction instructions maintained the original-case result, but a new variation exposed an identifier error that needed clearer rules. The final prompts met the evaluation requirements in all 15 runs for each original case. Next time, I would define evaluation rules earlier and test more unseen inputs, missing information, and ambiguous messages.
